# Privacy Policy

Enter Newline for AI does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal data.

The extension does not collect chat messages, prompts, browsing history, account information, or any other user content.

The extension uses browser local storage only to save its own settings, such as which supported AI services are enabled or disabled.

No data is sent to external servers.

This policy applies to the Enter Newline for AI browser extension.
